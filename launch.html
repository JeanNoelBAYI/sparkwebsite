A Few Things to Know

Who is the launch for?

Prospective mobility operators, businesses with fleets, campus and property teams, hospitality organisations, journalists, investors, and other mobility stakeholders.

Do I need an existing fleet to attend?

No. Whether you have an early idea or an established fleet, the launch is an introduction to Trekk and a chance to explore the possibilities.

When will the date and joining link be available?

The launch is confirmed for November 24, 2026 at 2:00 PM WAT (UTC+1). The platform and joining link are still being finalised and will be shared separately with registrants.

How can journalists request an interview?

Select “Media / journalist” when registering and include your request, or contact hello@trekkmobility.com. The team will coordinate availability directly.
