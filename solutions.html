Vehicle Compatibility

Vehicles Managed and Tracked by IoT Modules

Trekk’s hardware-agnostic IoT and telematics layer connects to the vehicle types your business already runs — or wants to launch next.

Personal Micromobility Fleets

Electric Scooters (E-Scooters)

The backbone of dockless fleets — Trekk’s IoT modules handle cellular lock/unlock, enforce geofenced speed limits, and send instant tip-over alerts to your team.

Electric Bicycles (E-Bikes)

Connected modules report live GPS location and battery charge, log motor fault codes for your field team, and trigger anti-theft wheel locks.

Electric Mopeds & Sitting Scooters

Trekk can configure smart helmet-compartment unlocking, remote ignition, and predictive maintenance schedules based on mileage and usage data.

Electric Skateboards & Onewheels

For consumer-grade fleets, Bluetooth-connected modules pair with the rider app to manage custom ride profiles and push firmware updates.

Urban Logistics & Cargo Vehicles

Electric Cargo Bikes

Courier and delivery fleets get live route tracking, driver safety scoring, and cargo-compartment lock status in the same dashboard as your passenger vehicles.

Autonomous Delivery Rovers

Trekk’s open APIs and telematics layer can ingest rover telemetry — location, battery, task status — alongside your other vehicles in one operator dashboard.

Low-Speed Vehicles (LSVs) & Neighborhood Electric Vehicles (NEVs)

Smart Golf Carts

Enforce strict geofenced boundaries so carts can’t leave resort or course grounds, with automatic speed limiting in sensitive zones.

Enclosed Micro-Cars

Trekk supports keyless entry, zone-based alerts, and configurable emergency-contact workflows as part of your vehicle connectivity setup.

Recreational & Water-Based Crafts

Electric Surfboards & Hydrofoils (eFoils)

IoT modules report battery level and ride telemetry, with automatic motor cut-off built into your hardware integration for rider safety.

Shared Jet Skis & Watercraft

Track rental sessions, set geofenced boundaries around swim zones, and route location alerts to your operations team for on-water incidents.
