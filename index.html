About us

Built for Operators

Spark Mobility gives shared-fleet operators the full technology stack — rider app, fleet dashboard, hardware connectivity, and payments — in one connected platform, so you can launch and scale a vehicle-sharing business without building software from scratch.

Mission

To give every operator, from a single-city scooter startup to a multi-country fleet, the same enterprise-grade technology — without the enterprise price tag or hardware lock-in.

Vision

A world where launching a shared mobility service takes weeks, not years, and every vehicle on the road is connected, tracked, and optimized in real time.

Why Choose Spark Mobility?

Launch in Weeks, Not Years

A white-label rider app, fleet dashboard, and payments stack ready to deploy — most operators go live in under a month.

Hardware-Agnostic Connectivity

Works with 30+ IoT and vehicle hardware vendors out of the box, so you’re never locked into one manufacturer.

Transparent, Operator-Friendly Pricing

No feature-gating and no hidden per-vehicle fees — scale from 10 vehicles to 10,000 on a plan that grows with you.

24/7 Fleet Support

Real humans monitoring uptime, payments, and rider issues around the clock — not a ticket queue.
