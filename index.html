About us

Built for Operators

We believe Africa deserves better mobility technology. Trekk gives shared-fleet operators the full technology stack — rider app, operator dashboard, vehicle connectivity, and payments — in one connected platform, so you can launch and scale a vehicle-sharing business without building software from scratch.

Mission

To accelerate the transition to smarter, more accessible and sustainable mobility across Africa — giving every operator, from a single-city startup to a multi-country fleet, enterprise-grade technology without the enterprise price tag or hardware lock-in.

Vision

A connected mobility ecosystem where every African city can access the technology to move people and goods more efficiently — launched in weeks, not years.
