Scooteasy Support

Need help with a ride?

The best way to reach Scooteasy support is through the mobile app, because your ride, scooter, payment, and account details are already attached to the request.

Use the Scooteasy mobile app

Open the Scooteasy mobile app and use the in-app support option to report ride issues, payment questions, scooter problems, accident reports, or account concerns.
